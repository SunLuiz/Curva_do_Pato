The Problem

“A trend without a significance test is an opinion.”

Raw monthly climate plots hide drift inside seasonal cycles — or trick users into mistaking noise for climate change.

Common Pitfalls We Solved

  • NASA POWER monthly endpoints include YYYY13 annual summary rows and -999.0 fill values that distort naive regressions.
  • Original Mann-Kendall loses power when summer/winter cycles dominate variance (e.g., Campinas solar irradiance p=0.20 raw vs p=8.6e-04 seasonal).
  • Dashboards often mix live APIs with mock layers without declaring data status.

Cockpit-OMNI Statistical Pipeline

  • Drops YYYY13 rows, aligns calendar PeriodIndex (freq=M).
  • Computes Seasonal Mann-Kendall (period=12) & Original MK (τ, p-value).
  • Calculates Deseasonalized Theil-Sen Slope per decade with 95% Confidence Interval and 42-year cumulative shift.
Methodology: Non-parametric rank correlation + robust median slope Slide 2 / 7
